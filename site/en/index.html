Research Interests

Molecular modeling and design

I study structure-aware models for molecular properties and decisions in molecular design, with a particular focus on cyclic-peptide permeability. My current work examines how chemical constraints, local edits, uncertainty, and limited measurement budgets affect candidate selection and sequential optimization.

Single-cell perturbation modeling and virtual cells

I develop and evaluate models of cellular responses to genetic and chemical perturbations. I am interested in how predictions transfer across interventions and cellular contexts, how they represent heterogeneous cell populations, and which measurements provide useful information for the next experiment.

Formal verification and checkable scientific reasoning

I investigate how formal methods can make AI-assisted scientific reasoning independently checkable. I develop approaches that combine neural theorem proving with formal proof checking, and explore applications of formal methods in chemistry.
